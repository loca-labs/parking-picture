Parking Picture — Support

How do I save my parking spot?

Open the app and tap the round shutter button. The photo appears in the bottom half of the screen with the time it was taken.

How do I zoom in on the photo?

Tap the photo to open it full screen, then pinch or double-tap to zoom. Tap the ✕ button to close.

The camera shows a permission message.

Tap "Grant camera access". If you denied it before, tap "Open Settings" and turn on Camera for Parking Picture.

Where is my photo stored?

Only on your device, inside the app. Nothing is uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.
