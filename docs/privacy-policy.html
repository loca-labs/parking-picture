Parking Picture — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Parking Picture ("the app") is made by Loca Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera access so you can take a photo of where you parked. Photos are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. The app keeps up to your 20 most recent photos; when you take more, the oldest is removed automatically. Photos are never uploaded, shared, or sent anywhere, and the app does not access your photo library.

Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not need an internet connection to work.

Deleting your data

You can delete any photo from within the app by opening it full screen and tapping Delete. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored photos from your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
