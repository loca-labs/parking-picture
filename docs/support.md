# Parking Picture — Support

Need help with Parking Picture? Find quick answers below or reach out to our team.

## How do I save my parking spot?

Open Parking Picture and tap the round white shutter button in the middle of the screen. Your photo immediately appears in the bottom pane with the time it was taken and a live elapsed parking timer.

## How do I use the flashlight in dark garages?

Tap the lightning bolt button (`⚡︎`) in the top-right corner of the live camera view to turn the flashlight on or off.

## How many photos does the app keep?

Parking Picture keeps up to your 20 most recent parking photos. Swipe left or right on the bottom pane (or in fullscreen view) to browse earlier photos. When you take more than 20 photos, the oldest one is automatically removed.

## How do I zoom in, share, or delete a photo?

Tap any saved photo to open it fullscreen. Pinch with two fingers or double-tap to zoom in on level numbers and signs. Use the bottom bar to **Share** the current photo, tap **Select** to share multiple photos at once, or tap **Delete** to remove a photo permanently.

## How do I add the Home Screen widget?

- **On iPhone:** Touch and hold an empty area on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle, tap **Edit** (or **+**) in the top-left corner, choose **Add Widget**, search for **Parking Picture**, and choose Small, Medium, or Large.
- **On Android:** Touch and hold an empty space on your Home Screen, tap **Widgets**, find **Parking Picture** ("Recent parking photos"), and drag it onto your Home Screen. You can resize it freely from 2×2 upward.

## The camera shows a permission message

Tap **Grant camera access**. If camera permission was previously denied, tap **Open Settings** and enable **Camera** for Parking Picture.

## Where are my photos stored?

Your parking photos are stored privately on your device inside the app and are never uploaded to any server. Read our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md) for full details.
