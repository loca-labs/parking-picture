# Parking Picture — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 20, 2026

Parking Picture ("the app") is developed by Loca Labs ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Parking Picture mobile application on iOS and Android.

## 1. Camera and Parking Photos (Stored Locally on Your Device)

- **Camera access:** The app requests access to your device's camera solely so you can photograph your parking spot and toggle the camera flashlight in low light.
- **On-device storage:** Photos you capture are stored only inside the app's private storage on your device (up to your 20 most recent photos; when you take more, the oldest photo is deleted automatically). Resized thumbnails of up to your 3 most recent photos are stored locally in the app's shared widget container on your device so the Home Screen widget can display them.
- **Never uploaded:** Your parking photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or backed up to Loca Labs servers or any third-party cloud service.
- **User-initiated sharing:** Photos leave the app only when you explicitly tap **Share** and select a destination (such as a messaging app or saving to your photo library) in your device's system share sheet. On iOS, photo library access is requested only if you choose to save a shared photo to your library.

## 2. Diagnostics, Crash Reporting, and App Performance

To keep the app reliable and fix crashes quickly, Parking Picture uses **Expo Observe** (provided by 650 Industries, Inc. / Expo as our data processor) to collect anonymous technical diagnostics and app health telemetry over encrypted HTTPS connections:

- **Crash logs and errors:** JavaScript exceptions, stack traces, and native crash signals.
- **Performance and device diagnostics:** App launch and render timings, app version and build number, operating system name and version, device model, language tag, and startup battery/thermal/network state.
- **Anonymous installation identifier & coarse region:** A randomly generated app-installation client ID, per-launch session ID, and country-level region inferred from the network request IP address.
- **Anonymous feature interaction counts:** Non-identifying app events (such as whether a photo was captured, deleted, or shared, photo count, or whether the flashlight was toggled). These events never include your photos, filenames, GPS coordinates, or personal identity.

We do **not** require user accounts, do **not** collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise GPS location, do **not** display advertising, and do **not** track you across other apps or websites.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion

- **Parking photos:** Kept only on your device. You can delete individual photos at any time by opening a photo fullscreen and tapping **Delete**. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored photos, widget thumbnails, and the local installation identifier from your device.
- **Diagnostic records:** Diagnostic and crash records processed by Expo Observe are retained for 90 days (up to 93 days while scheduled background deletion completes) and are then permanently deleted.
- **Deletion requests:** If you have questions or wish to submit a data inquiry, email us at [contact@loca-labs.com](mailto:contact@loca-labs.com).

## 4. Data Sharing and Security

All diagnostic transmissions are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Diagnostic data is processed solely on our behalf by Expo under a service-provider data processing agreement and is never sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Parking Picture is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.
